WHO WE ARE

What is NTHS?

The National Technical Honor Society recognizes students who demonstrate excellence in their technical education, academics, leadership, and character.

NTHS provides students with opportunities to grow as leaders, develop professional skills, serve their communities, and prepare for their future careers.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

To honor student achievement and leadership while encouraging members to continue developing the skills needed for success in their education, careers, and communities.

OUR FUTURE

Our Vision

To empower the next generation of skilled professionals and leaders through technical education, service, and opportunity.
