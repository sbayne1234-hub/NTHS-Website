Membership

Join a community that celebrates technical achievement, leadership, and career readiness.

WHO CAN JOIN?

Can you do it?

NTHS membership recognizes students who have demonstrated excellence in their career and technical education programs and are committed to leadership and personal growth.

MEMBERSHIP REQUIREMENTS

What Does It Take?

01

Academic Excellence

Maintain a minimum unweighted GPA of 3.0 or higher.

02

Technical Achievement

Demonstrate commitment and achievement in your career and technical education program by obtaining at least 1 high school certification.

03

Academic Commitment

Must have been in and passed TWO CTE classes.

04

Character

Demonstrate good character and commitment in your CTE classes.
